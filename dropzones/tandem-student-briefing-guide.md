# Tandem Student Briefing Guide

**For:** Tandem instructors and dropzone S&TA use  
**Duration:** 20–30 minutes ground school

---

## Equipment Introduction (5 min)

- Show student the tandem container system
- Point out: main canopy, reserve, AAD
- Explain the instructor's role — they control everything, student's job is body position
- Fit harness: leg straps, chest strap, explain why snug matters

## Body Position (10 min)

**The arch:**
- Hips forward, head up, shoulders back
- Arms out at shoulder height, bent at 90°
- Legs bent at 45°, feet up toward backside
- Practice on the ground — correct until natural

**What not to do:**
- Reach for the instructor
- Look straight down
- Tense up and go rigid

**Exits:** Explain the door, the step, the count ("ready, set, go" or your DZ standard). Practice the count.

## Freefall (3 min)

- Duration: approximately 60 seconds from 15,000 ft
- Altimeter: point it out, explain what it reads
- Altitude for pull: instructor handles it, but student should know ~5,500 ft
- What it feels like: wind noise, not falling — flying

## Canopy (5 min)

- Deployment: instructor pulls, brief surge, then silence
- Harness check: leg straps, toggles out
- Toggles: demonstrate flare motion — full up, full down, steer
- Landing: student lifts legs, instructor lands — feet up until told otherwise
- Landing area: point it out from the ground

## Questions

- Address any fear or anxiety directly — normalize it
- Ask if student has any medical conditions not on waiver (last check)
- Confirm student understands they control nothing except body position

## Before Loading

- [ ] Harness fit confirmed (second check)
- [ ] Goggles fitted
- [ ] Altimeter on wrist, oriented
- [ ] Student name confirmed on manifest
- [ ] Waiver and video release signed

---

*This guide is a framework. Your DZ's S&TA and chief instructor have final authority on briefing content.*
