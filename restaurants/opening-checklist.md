# Restaurant Opening Checklist

**For:** Restaurant managers and shift leads  
**When:** 60–90 minutes before first seating

---

## Before Staff Arrives

- [ ] Alarm disarmed, lights on
- [ ] Walk the dining room — check for overnight issues (pests, water, HVAC)
- [ ] Check refrigeration temperatures (log if required by health code)
- [ ] Review reservation sheet for the day

## Kitchen

- [ ] Line temps verified — walk-in cooler ≤38°F, freezer ≤0°F
- [ ] Prep list reviewed with kitchen lead
- [ ] 86 list cleared or updated
- [ ] Dish area set up, sanitizer solution at correct concentration
- [ ] Date labels on all prepped items

## Front of House

- [ ] Tables set per standard (linens, silverware, glassware)
- [ ] Host stand stocked: menus, reservation list, pens
- [ ] POS terminals on and tested — all logged in
- [ ] Bar restocked: garnish, ice, liquor pulls verified
- [ ] Server sections assigned and posted

## Staff Briefing (15 min before open)

- [ ] 86 items communicated to all servers
- [ ] Daily specials reviewed with prices
- [ ] Any large parties or special events noted
- [ ] Shift roles confirmed (expo, food runner, bar support)

## Last Check Before Doors Open

- [ ] Sign flipped / Open indicator lit
- [ ] Music on at appropriate level
- [ ] Host at stand
- [ ] Kitchen confirmed ready

---

*Timing and steps will vary by concept. Adjust to your operation.*
